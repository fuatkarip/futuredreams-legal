Privacy Policy

Game: Last Landing on Mars (in German: Letzte Landung auf dem Mars, in Turkish: Mars’a Son İniş) · Android and iOS · Last updated: 11 November 2026

Who is responsible

FutureDreams Games (Fuat Karip). Contact for all privacy questions: support@solarisnetwork.de

Summary

  • No account, no name, no e-mail, no advertising ID, no location tracking.
  • Your progress and settings stay on your device.
  • Anonymous usage data and crash reports are only sent if you allow it. You can change this at any time in Settings → "Anonymous usage data".
  • The game has no ads and no in-app purchases.

Data stored only on your device

Game progress (missions landed, stars, deepest pad in the pit, gold, story tablets, chosen captain and ship parts), settings (language, sound, pilot voice, your consent answer) and a local gameplay log used for testing. This data never leaves your device unless you allow anonymous usage data (see below). Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Anonymous usage data and crash reports (only with your consent)

When you first start the game we ask whether we may collect anonymous usage data and crash reports. Only if you tap "Allow" do we use the following Google services:

ServiceWhat is processedWhy
Firebase Analytics (Google)Gameplay events (e.g. mission or pit level started/finished, landing result, depth reached, pickups, gold earned/spent), a random app-instance ID, device model, operating system and app version, language, and an approximate country derived from the IP address.To see which missions and pit levels are too hard or too easy and to balance the game.
Firebase Crashlytics (Google)Crash reports: technical stack traces, device model, operating system and app version, a random installation ID.To find and fix crashes.

The advertising ID is never collected, and all advertising-related consent signals are set to "denied". The data cannot be used to identify you by name. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Analytics event data is kept for 2 months, crash reports for 90 days.

Provider: Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, and Google LLC (USA). Data may be transferred to the USA; Google is certified under the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and uses the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. More: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Withdrawing consent

Open Settings in the game and switch "Anonymous usage data" off. Collection stops immediately. Data already sent is deleted after the retention periods above; on request we delete it earlier.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and to object, and the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. Write to support@solarisnetwork.de.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not ask for personal data. Usage data is only collected with consent given in the game.

Changes

If what we collect changes, we update this page and ask for your consent again in the game.
