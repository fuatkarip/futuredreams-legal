Privacy Policy

Game: Wordy Bus (Turkish: Kelime Dolmuşu, German: Wörtertram) · Android and iOS · Last updated: 25 November 2026

Who is responsible

FutureDreams Games (Fuat Karip). Contact for all privacy questions: support@solarisnetwork.de

Summary

  • No account, no name, no e-mail, no location tracking.
  • Your progress, tickets and settings stay on your device.
  • Anonymous usage data and crash reports are only sent if you allow it; change it any time in Settings → "Anonymous usage data".
  • The only ads are optional videos that you start yourself for a bonus. No banners, no ads that interrupt the game.
  • In-app purchases are handled by Apple or Google; we never see your payment data.

Data stored only on your device

Progress (levels, stars, daily puzzles, streak, unfinished puzzle, tickets, hints), what you own, settings and a local gameplay log. This data never leaves your device unless you allow anonymous usage data. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Anonymous usage data and crash reports (only with your consent)

On first start we ask whether we may collect them. Only if you tap "Allow" do we use:

ServiceWhat is processedWhy
Firebase Analytics (Google)Gameplay events (level or daily started, solved or lost, mistakes and the puzzle word involved, hints, offers, purchases without payment data, frame rate), a random app-instance ID, device model, operating system and app version, language and an approximate country derived from the IP address.To see which puzzles are too hard, too easy or unfair.
Firebase Crashlytics (Google)Crash reports: technical stack traces, device model, operating system and app version, a random installation ID.To find and fix crashes.

Analytics never uses the advertising ID; its advertising consent signals are always "denied". Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Event data is kept for 2 months, crash reports for 90 days. Provider: Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, and Google LLC (USA), certified under the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework. More: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Optional reward videos (Google AdMob)

From level 3 on you can choose to watch a short video for a bonus (extra bench seat, second chance, hint, undo, double tickets, a missed day). A video only plays when you tap its button. Google AdMob then processes the advertising ID (on iOS only if you allow tracking), device and app information, IP address and how you interact with the ad, to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud.

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland Google's consent form asks you first; you can refuse, and change your choice in Settings → "Ad choices".
  • On iOS the system asks whether the game may track you; if you say no, you only get non-personalised ads.
  • Ad content is limited to the general rating (G).

Provider: Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC. More: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We only receive the confirmation that a product was bought (product and transaction number), which stays on your device to unlock it. Legal basis: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Withdrawing consent and your rights

Switch "Anonymous usage data" off in Settings; collection stops immediately. You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection, and to complain to a supervisory authority. Write to support@solarisnetwork.de.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages but not directed at children, and asks for no personal data.

Changes

If what we collect changes, we update this page and ask again in the game.
