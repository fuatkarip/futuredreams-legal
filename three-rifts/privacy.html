Privacy Policy

Game: Three Rifts · Android and iOS · Last updated: 20 November 2026

Who is responsible

FutureDreams Games (Fuat Karip). Contact for all privacy questions: support@solarisnetwork.de

Summary

  • The game creates a player account for you automatically, tied to a random device ID. You don't have to give a name, e-mail or password.
  • Your player name, profile picture choice, gold, cosmetics, match results and statistics, friends, chat messages and reports are stored on our own game server so that friends, chat and rankings work.
  • Linking Apple or Google is optional. It lets you keep your account on a new device.
  • Anonymous usage data and crash reports are only sent if you allow it. You can change this at any time in Profile → Settings.
  • No in-app purchases. Cosmetics are bought with gold earned in the game. The only ads are optional videos that you start yourself for a bonus (rewarded videos, Google AdMob).

Optional reward videos (Google AdMob)

After your first finished match the game can offer a short video: to double a match reward, to open the daily chest, or to come back at once after falling in a match against bots. A video only plays when you tap its button. To show these videos we use Google AdMob, which processes the advertising ID (only if you allow tracking), device and app information, IP address and how you interact with the ad, to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud.

  • On iOS the system asks first, when the game starts for the first time, whether the game may track you; if you say no, you only get non-personalised ads.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland Google's consent form asks you before any ad is requested. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) where required.
  • Ad content is limited to suit a general audience (rating PG).

Provider: Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC. More: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Deleting your account and data

E-mail support@solarisnetwork.de with the subject "Three Rifts account deletion" and your friend code (Profile). We delete your account, profile, statistics, friends and messages within 30 days. Switching off "Share usage data" in Profile → Settings stops analytics at once. More on the support page.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and to object, and the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. Write to support@solarisnetwork.de.

Children

The game is meant for players aged 13 and over. Chat is limited to friends and filtered. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes

If what we collect changes, we update this page and, where needed, ask for your consent again in the game.
