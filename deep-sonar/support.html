Gizlilik politikası

Delete your data · Daten löschen · Verilerini sil

Deep Sonar: Submarine Dive (FutureDreams Games). 1) In the game open Settings and switch "Anonymous data" off: nothing is sent any more. 2) To delete data already sent, e-mail support@solarisnetwork.de with the subject "Deep Sonar data deletion". We delete the analytics and crash data of your installation within 30 days. Data on your device (progress, settings) is deleted when you uninstall the game. Without a request, analytics event data is deleted after 2 months and crash reports after 90 days. No account exists, so nothing else is stored.
